## Big finding: the current SSC deal list is publicly readable

The Starving Student Card web app has a public demo page at https://app.starvingstudentcard.com/preview. That page loads its deals from `POST /data/sort` (a Laravel app that needs an XSRF cookie from the preview page). I queried it with Orem center (40.2969, -111.6946). It returned **620 active deals**, each with:
- the business name
- the deal title
- the business location coordinates (`_geo`)
- the distance

Several Orem deals have `new_until` set around Nov 2026, so they were added in about Oct 2026. The page carries the banner **"FOR DEMO PURPOSES ONLY"**. The deals look like the real current set, but that is not certain. That is why every `ssc` field stays `sscConfirmed: false` until Tim's screenshots match it.

- Raw dump: `ssc-preview-deals-orem-2026-10-15.json`, in this scratchpad.
- Readable grouping of everything within 13 km: `near.txt`.
- Each spot's `sscSource` says whether its text came from this app data or from the old card. Every included `ssc` value came from the app data.
- Deal wording is copied from the app and lightly shortened. Where the 2022 card added a restriction (e.g. "excludes buffet", "Mon-Thu only"), I appended it in parentheses.

**Caution: an SSC listing does not prove a business is open.** Paleta Bar Provo and Crispy Cones Provo are both still in the app, and both have closed. So `verifiedOpen: true` always rests on separate dated 2025-26 evidence, recorded in `openEvidence`. The 4 "unknown" businesses are the ones whose only evidence was the SSC listing.

## How coordinates were made (`coordsSource`)

All sources are free and need no API key.

- `ssc-app`: the location stored in the SSC app data. I reverse-geocoded every one with OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
  - The first text variant means OSM names that exact business at that point, so it is very reliable.
  - The second variant means no named POI was found. For those I geocoded the street address and compared: most agree within 70 m.
  - Where they disagreed by more than 120 m and the street grid supported the address, I switched to the address point instead: LoLo, Rumbi, Roxberry, Ivie.
- `osm-poi`: a named OpenStreetMap feature (business, park, trailhead, theatre) found by Nominatim search.
- `osm-address`: an OSM/Utah address point for the exact house number.
- `osm-node`: the Kyhv Peak Overlook viewpoint, OSM node 415803094.
- `approx`: a mall or street point, possibly 100-300 m off. Used for:
  - JJ Coffee (Park Ave street segment)
  - The Orchard and University Place holiday lights (mall point)
  - Alpine Loop (Sundance stop on SR-92; it is a drive, not a point)

Known coordinate soft spots:
- **Padel Den:** OSM has no feature yet. I used the SSC point; the street geocode was about 400 m away on the wrong street.
- **The Rift:** I used the SSC point. It is inside Provo Towne Centre mall.
- **Habit Burger Provo:** kept the SSC point, which OSM names as the Habit. The address point was 445 m away, probably a parcel centroid.

## Biggest uncertainties

1. **Prices are mostly estimates.**
   - Food, dessert and drinks: every `costPerCouple` is my estimate of a normal order for two, labeled "estimate" in `priceNote`. No official menu prices were reachable.
   - Activities: these mostly use official or recent published prices, and `priceNote` says which.
   - None of the costs subtract the SSC deal. That is for the planner code to do.
2. **SSC deals are from a page marked "FOR DEMO".** Tim's screenshots are the source of truth. Restrictions such as days, dine-in only or max value are often missing from the app titles.
3. **Some activity prices are dated or only partial.** Classic Fun Center's site may show its Sandy location's prices. Hang Time and Rhyno's prices come from directory pages 10 months to 3 years old. Karaoke38 publishes no room rate.
4. **Marnell's Country Dance location.** The official site says 488 N 800 E, Provo, and that is what I used. The SSC app pins it at 765 S Orem Blvd, Orem.
5. **Hours.** Many are "about" values from aggregators. For a few, only some days were found. 9 spots have `hours: null`.
6. **Drive times** were not computed. Distances can be calculated from lat/lng.
   - Everything is within about 13 km of Orem center, except the Alpine Loop point (14 km, a drive).
   - The Provo Towne Centre places (The Rift) and The Ruth / Hee Haw Farms (Pleasant Grove) are at the roughly 10-12 minute edge.
