# Perfect Boyfriend

A private iPhone-first web app for keeping a relationship on track: important dates with calendar reminders, everything you know about your partner, gift ideas, a private log of recurring arguments and what fixed them, and cheap Orem, Utah date plans built from nearby spots and Starving Student Card deals.

Open it at https://timothyhadfield.github.io/perfect-boyfriend/ (add it to your iPhone home screen from Safari's Share menu). Run locally with `python -m http.server 8765` in this folder. Data lives in your own Firebase account (project `perfect-boyfriend-th`), never in this repo.
